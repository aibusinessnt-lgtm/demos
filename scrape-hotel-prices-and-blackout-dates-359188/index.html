Scrape Hotel Prices and Blackout Dates

Get accurate hotel prices and blackout dates collected automatically, without checking each site by hand.

How this works

I'd start by confirming which hotel pages and rate sources hold your prices and blackout dates today. Then a scraper built with Claude, plus a scheduled job, collects the rates and date restrictions from each page. Claude helps read messy page layouts and tidy them into one clean list. Anything that looks odd gets flagged for a quick human check. Click through the demo to see a pretend run with sample data.
