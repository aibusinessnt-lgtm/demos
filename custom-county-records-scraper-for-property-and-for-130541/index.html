Custom County Records Scraper for Property and Foreclosure Leads

Get clean, deduplicated county property and foreclosure lead lists delivered automatically, without visiting each county site.

How this works

I'd build this in Python with a browser automation tool, with each county as its own small plug-in so adding a new one never means rebuilding anything. The demo is a pretend run: first we map how each county site works today, then it searches, pulls the property details, cleans and removes duplicates, and exports. Jobs are logged, rate-limited and can resume where they stopped, and I respect each site's terms with no workarounds for logins or CAPTCHAs. You'd get the source code, documentation, and operating instructions, plus a simple screen to start and watch runs.

1. Map each county site

Before any code, I review how the county's property search works today: what you can search by, what each record page shows, and the site's rules and limits. Each county gets its own small plug-in.

Sample County A: search by owner, address or parcel. Record page shows value, taxes and sale history.

2. Search and filter

The bot searches by owner name, address or parcel number, or filters by property type, value and location. Large batches are worked through at a polite pace.

Batch of 250 parcels queued for Sample County A

3. Extract property details

For each record it collects owner, mailing address, parcel number, values, taxes, homestead status, year built, size, sale history and any available mortgage details.

Records are standardized into consistent columns and repeats are merged, then saved to a database.

5. Export and schedule

Results go to Excel, CSV and Google Sheets. The job reruns on a schedule across all counties, logs any errors, and resumes where it left off if interrupted.

A simple screen lets you start a job, see progress by county, and review the log.
