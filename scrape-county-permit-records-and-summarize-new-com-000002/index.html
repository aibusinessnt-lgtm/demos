Scrape County Permit Records and Summarize New Commercial Projects Weekly

How this works

I'm showing a pretend weekly run with made-up sample permits, and you can step through it one stage at a time. A scheduled job built with Claude and Python visits each county site, with custom handling for the older, clunkier ones. It filters to commercial permits over $250k and drops anything already seen before. Claude then summarizes each project and scores it as a roofing lead, and the ranked list goes out to your sales team every Monday morning.

1. Scheduled scrape of 12 county sites

Sunday night, a Python scraper visits each county permit site. Modern sites use simple requests, and the old clunky ones use a headless browser with retries. If a site fails, it is flagged instead of silently skipped.

Counties checked: 12 | Succeeded: 11 | Retried: 1 | Raw permits pulled: 214

2. Filter to commercial projects over $250k

Residential permits and small jobs are removed using permit type and declared project value.

214 raw permits → 37 commercial permits over $250k

3. Remove duplicates

Each permit is matched against last weeks' records by permit number and county, with an address check as backup. Only new ones continue.

37 candidates → 9 already seen → 28 new permits

4. Claude summarizes and scores each project

Claude reads the permit description and writes a plain summary, then scores roofing lead potential from 1 to 100 with a short reason.

5. Rank the list

Projects are sorted by score. Top leads include the address, owner or contractor name from the permit, value and a one-line summary.

28 scored → Top 10 hot leads, 11 medium, 7 low

6. Monday 7:00 AM delivery to sales

The ranked list is sent to the sales team as a clean email and saved to a shared sheet, so nothing depends on one person's day of manual checking.
