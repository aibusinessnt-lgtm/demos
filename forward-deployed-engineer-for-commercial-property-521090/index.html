Forward Deployed Engineer for Commercial Property Management: AI Apps & Integration

Fill more units and collect more rent on time with AI tools that fit your current systems.

How this works

I'd start by sitting with your property team and mapping how leasing and rent collection actually happen today, so we only fix problems worth fixing. The first flow handles leasing: posting units, answering inquiries fast, booking tours and following up. The second flow is separate and handles rent collection: spotting overdue balances early and following up consistently. Claude drafts the replies and reminders, and your team stays in control of anything sensitive. A simple scorecard tracks lead response time, occupancy and on-time rent so you can see what is working.
