How this works

I'm showing a sketch of one system built with Claude plus the tools your team already uses, split into two tracks: leasing and rent collection. On the leasing side, every inquiry gets a fast, helpful reply, a tour gets booked, and prospects get followed up automatically. On the rent side, a daily check of balances flags overdue accounts early and sends the right reminder, and anything sensitive goes to a person. Everything logs to a simple metrics table so you can see lead response time, occupancy and on-time rent over time. The first step in a real project would be sitting with your team to map what actually happens today, so I'd build around that.
