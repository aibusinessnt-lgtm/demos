AI Integration Consultant for Business

Smoother workflows and better team collaboration through the right AI tools in Notion and Slack.

How this works

I start by mapping how your team works today, so we only automate what is worth it. Then I recommend the right tools, mainly Notion, Slack and Claude, and show how they connect. The flow here shows one example, where a Slack request becomes a Notion task with an AI summary. I would roll it out in small steps with your team, then keep improving it as an ongoing project.

Step-by-step approach

01Discover how work happens todayProcess review
Short chats with your team and a look at current tools to map where time is lost, such as repeated questions, scattered notes and manual updates.
Result: a simple map of current processes and the biggest time-wasters
↓
02Recommend the right toolsClaude
I match each problem to a tool, using Notion for knowledge and tasks, Slack for conversation and Claude for summaries and drafts. Nothing is added unless it earns its place.
Result: a short, prioritized tool plan with reasons
↓
03Team asks for work in SlackSlack
A team member posts a request in a shared channel, like they already do today.
↓
04AI summarizes and sorts itClaude
Claude reads the message, writes a clear summary and decides the type of request and its urgency.
Result: Request summarized as a client report draft, due Friday
↓
05Is it urgent?Decision
Urgent items get flagged right away. Everything else goes into the normal queue.
→ Urgent
06Notify owner in SlackSlack
The right person gets a direct message with the summary.
→ Not urgent
07Add to the weekly queueNotion
The task is created in the Notion board with owner, due date and summary.
↓
08Create the Notion taskNotion
Every request ends up in one place in Notion, so nothing gets lost in chat.
Result: one organized task board the whole team can see
↓
09Weekly digest to the teamClaude
Each week Claude posts a short Slack summary of what was done, what is stuck and what is next.
↓
10Guide rollout and measureOngoing support
I train the team, collect feedback and adjust the setup, then suggest the next improvement.
Result: steady, low-risk gains in productivity and teamwork
