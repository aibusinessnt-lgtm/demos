Facebook Marketplace Automation with n8n, AI Agents and Python

Faster customer replies, organized listings, and stock and orders tracked automatically for your phone accessories shop.

How this works

I'd start by reviewing how you handle listings, messages and orders today, and checking which Marketplace features Meta officially supports for automation. This board shows that as the first step, then three separate processes: customer inquiries, inventory and orders, and listing updates. In n8n, an AI model drafts answers to price, availability, compatibility and delivery questions from your sheet, and anything tricky goes to you for approval. Google Sheets is the single source of truth for stock, with low-stock alerts, order status updates and follow-ups, plus error logging and a handover guide.
