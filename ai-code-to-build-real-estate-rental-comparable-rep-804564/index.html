AI Code to Build Real Estate Rental Comparable Report

Turn rent comp reports from hours of manual work into a repeatable, branded process taking minutes.

How this works

I built this as a system using Claude plus a few connected services, since your report template already lives in Claude. The flow starts by mapping how your team builds the report today, then pulls in your PDFs, Excel files, lead stats and Rentometer data. Photos are matched to each comp, a branded report is produced, and everything is filed by building and unit so you can re-run it weekly or twice a month. My recommendation is a lightweight app on top of Claude, because 100+ buildings and 500+ units need folders and scheduling that a single Claude Project handles poorly. I'd cover costs, photo sourcing and licensing, timeline and quote in my proposal.

Building the rent comp report

01Map today's processDiscovery
We walk through how you and your assistant pick comps, verify data and prompt Claude today, and review your current report template. This shows what to keep and what to automate.
Result: a clear map of every manual step and handoff
↓
02Upload comp filesSimple upload page
Your team drops in PDF exports from Rentometer, the MLS and HelloData, plus any Excel files and the Zillow and Apartments.com lead stats (calls and tours).
↓
03Pull Rentometer data directlyRentometer API
Comp data comes straight from the Rentometer connection, with HelloData's connection added later. This means less exporting and re-typing.
↓
04Extract and check the compsClaude
Claude reads each PDF and spreadsheet and pulls out address, rent, size and unit details. Anything unclear or missing is flagged for your assistant to verify rather than guessed.
Result: one clean comp table with flagged items to review
↓
05Anything flagged?Review check
The assistant approves or corrects flagged items before the report is built.
→ All data looks good
06Continue to photosAuto
The report moves on with no extra work.
→ Something is missing or unclear
07Quick human reviewAssistant
The assistant fixes or adds comps the tools missed, then the report continues.
↓
08Find photos for each compPhoto sourcing
The system searches licensed listing sources and public images for each comp address and matches the photo to the right comp. Anything it can't find or isn't allowed to use is marked so you can swap it in.
↓
09Build the branded reportClaude + template
Claude fills your template with comps, photos and lead stats, and produces a consistent branded PDF every time.
↓
10Save by building and unitOrganized storage
Each report is filed in a folder for its building and unit, so 100+ buildings and 500+ units stay easy to find.
↓
11Re-run and send on a scheduleScheduler
Reports re-run weekly or twice a month with fresh data and are sent out automatically. Your team just clicks a button, and a short guide or walkthrough video covers it.
Result: updated reports delivered on schedule
